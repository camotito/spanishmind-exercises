Time, days and prices

With the time we use the verb "ser".
Only two forms of this verb are needed for the time:

  • it/she/he is = es
  • they are = son

Time

La hora = the hour
¿Qué hora es? = What time is it?

Son las dos. = It's two o'clock.
Literally translated it means "They are the two".
The word hora is understood and it is not said.

  • Es la una. = It's one o'clock. (one hour: singular)
  • Son las dos. = It's two o'clock. (two hours: plural)
  • Son las ocho. = It's eight o'clock.

Only la una is singular: la (the).
Every other hour is plural: las (the).

Minutes

The English and Spanish structures are the same here:

  • Son las tres y veinte. = It's three and twenty (3:20).
  • Es la una y veinticinco. = It's one and twenty-five (1:25).
  • Son las cuatro menos diez. = It's four minus ten (3:50).

Menos = minus.

Two times have their own word:

y cuarto = and a quarter:

  • Son las once y cuarto. = It's a quarter past eleven. (11:15)

y media = and half:

  • Son las ocho y media. = It's half past eight. (8:30)

After half past, Spanish speakers usually count back to the next hour with menos:

  • Es la una menos cuarto. = It's a quarter to one. (12:45)

At what time?

¿A qué hora? = At what time?
The answer uses a (at).
And the most important difference with English: Spanish keeps la and las.

  • La fiesta es a las nueve. = The party is at nine.
  • El partido es a las diez. = The match is at ten.
  • Nos vemos a la una. = We'll see each other at one.

Compare:
Son las tres = It is three.
A las tres = At three. (literally: at the three)

Common mistakes:

  • ✗ en las tres
  • ✗ a tres

Correct:

  • ✓ a las tres

Morning, afternoon, night

  • las siete de la mañana = 7 in the morning (literally: the seven of the morning)
  • las tres de la tarde = 3 in the afternoon
  • las diez de la noche = 10 at night

To say in the morning as a habit, use por (by) + la:

  • por la mañana = in the morning (literally: by the morning)
  • por la tarde = in the afternoon
  • por la noche = at night

Examples:

  • Normalmente estudiamos por la noche. = We usually study at night.
  • Trabajo por la tarde. = I work in the afternoon.

Days

The one key difference in Spanish when talking about days:

  • There is no word for on with days. Instead it uses el (the) for one particular day and los (plural the) for every week.

Examples:

  • Viajo el lunes. = I travel on Monday. (literally: I travel the Monday)
  • Salgo los sábados. = I go out on Saturdays. (literally: I go out the Saturdays)
  • Hoy es jueves. = Today is Thursday.

Monday to Friday do not change in the plural, only el becomes los:

  • el martes = on Tuesday
  • los martes = on Tuesdays

Both are correct:

  • El martes tenemos clase. = We have class on Tuesday.
  • Los martes tenemos clase. = We have class on Tuesdays.

Saturday and Sunday add -s:

  • el sábado = on Saturday
  • los sábados = on Saturdays
  • el domingo = on Sunday
  • los domingos = on Sundays

Common mistake:

  • ✗ en lunes

Correct:

  • ✓ el lunes

Dates

  • Mi cumpleaños es el doce de febrero. = My birthday is on the 12th of February.
  • Nací el dos de diciembre. = I was born on the 2nd of December. (literally: I was born the two of December)

With a day and a date together, el goes before the day only:

  • Felipe se casa el martes 13 de julio. = Felipe is getting married on Tuesday 13th July.

How often

A + el / la works like the English per.

  • Dos veces a la semana. = Twice a week. (literally: twice at the week)
  • Duermo ocho horas al día. = I sleep eight hours a day.
  • Voy al gimnasio dos días a la semana. = I go to the gym two days a week.
  • Vamos de vacaciones una vez al año. = We go on holiday once a year.

A + el always becomes al:

  • al día = a day
  • al mes = a month
  • al año = a year

With la nothing changes:

  • a la semana = a week

Prices

For per with prices and measures, Spanish uses just el (the), la or los, with no a.

  • Dos euros el kilo. = Two euros a kilo. (literally: two euros the kilo)
  • Un euro el litro. = One euro a litre.
  • Dos euros la docena. = Two euros a dozen.
  • Dos euros los cien gramos. = Two euros per 100 grams.
