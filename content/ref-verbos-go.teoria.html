tengo, pongo, traigo

Verbs with a g

Some verbs get a g when you talk about yourself.

  • tengo = I have
  • vengo = I come
  • salgo = I go out
  • pongo = I put
  • supongo = I suppose
  • traigo = I bring

Examples:

  • Pongo mi cartera en la mesa. = I put my wallet on the table.
  • Siempre traigo el ordenador a clase. = I always bring the computer to class.

-pose and -tain

English verbs with -pose are Spanish verbs with -poner.
They have the same g: pongo, supongo.

  • poner = to put
  • componer = to compose
  • suponer = to suppose
  • proponer = to propose (a plan, not marriage)

English verbs with -tain are Spanish verbs with -tener.

  • contener = to contain
  • mantener = to maintain
  • obtener = to obtain
