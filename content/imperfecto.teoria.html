How things were: the imperfect

Use it for how things were, or what was happening, with no clear start or end.

  • used to / was ...-ing
  • Imperfect = not finished.
  • Llovía mucho. / It was raining a lot.
  • Había mucha gente. / There were a lot of people.
  • Cuando era niño, vivía en una casa pequeña. / When I was a child, I lived in a small house.

Always imperfect

  • solía ser / it used to be
  • iba a / I was going to

A story has two parts

The scene, how things were: imperfect.

  • era / it was
  • vivía / I lived, I used to live
  • llovía / it was raining
  • había / there was, there were

The events, one thing after another: preterite.

  • fui / I went
  • compré / I bought
  • vi / I saw
  • Era de noche y llovía. / It was night and it was raining.
  • Entré en un bar. / I went into a bar.
  • Había mucha gente. / There were a lot of people.
  • Vi a un amigo. / I saw a friend.
  • Cuando era niño, vivía cerca del mar. / When I was a child, I lived near the sea.
  • Un día, mi padre compró un piano. / One day, my father bought a piano.
