tener hambre, sed, frío, calor

In Spanish you have hunger, thirst, cold and heat.

  • tengo hambre = I'm hungry (literally: I have hunger)
  • tengo sed = I'm thirsty (literally: I have thirst)
  • tengo frío = I'm cold (think frigid)
  • tengo calor = I'm hot (think calorie)
  • tengo miedo = I'm afraid (literally: I have fear)

Examples:

  • Mi amigo tiene sed. = My friend is thirsty.
  • Tengo mucha hambre. = I'm very hungry. (literally: I have a lot of hunger)

tener ganas de

tener ganas de + verb = to feel like + verb with -ing (literally: to have desires of).

ganas is always plural, with an s, and de comes after it.

Examples:

  • Tengo ganas de cenar. = I feel like having dinner.
  • ¿Qué tienes ganas de hacer esta noche? = What do you feel like doing tonight?

Typical mistakes:

  • Tengo gana de cenar. → incorrect
  • Tengo ganas de cenar. → correct

hace calor, hace frío

For the weather, Spanish uses hace (it makes).

  • hace calor = it's hot (literally: it makes heat)
  • hace frío = it's cold (literally: it makes cold)
  • hace 20 grados = it's 20 degrees

Examples:

  • Hoy hace frío. = Today it's cold.
  • Hace frío y no quiero ir. = It's cold and I don't want to go.

Typical mistakes:

  • Hoy es frío. → incorrect
  • Hoy hace frío. → correct

tengo frío is you. hace frío is the weather.
