tengo que or debo?

tengo que = I have to

An obligation that comes from outside: your schedule, rules, something that needs to be done.

  • Tengo que trabajar mañana. / I have to work tomorrow.
  • Tengo que ir a casa. / I have to go home.
  • Tengo que llegar temprano. / I have to arrive early.

debo = I must

A duty that comes from inside: what you believe is right.

Debo = I owe (think debt, debit). You owe it to other people or to yourself.

  • Debo decir la verdad. / I must tell the truth.
  • Debo respetar a mi familia. / I must respect my family.
  • Debo ser honesto con mis amigos. / I must be honest with my friends.

How to choose

  • The reason is outside you (a schedule, a place, a task): tengo que.
  • The reason is a duty you feel is right: debo.

Heads-up

In everyday Spanish, speakers often use tengo que for personal duties too.

Debo sounds more serious.
