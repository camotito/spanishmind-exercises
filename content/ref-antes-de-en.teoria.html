antes de, cerca de, en

antes de, después de

antes de = before.

después de = after.

In English you can say before eating. In Spanish you need de (of), and the verb ends in -r.

  • antes de cenar = before having dinner
  • después de trabajar = after working

Examples:

  • Antes de cenar tengo que ir al banco. = Before having dinner I have to go to the bank.
  • Leo antes de dormir. = I read before sleeping.
  • Después de comer siempre voy a casa en tren. = After eating I always go home by train.

Typical mistakes:

  • Antes cenar tengo que ir al banco. → incorrect
  • Antes de cenar tengo que ir al banco. → correct

cerca de, lejos de

These words also take de before what comes next.

de + el = del, like a + el = al.

  • cerca de = near (literally: near of)
  • lejos de = far from

Examples:

  • El parque está cerca del banco. = The park is near the bank.
  • Mi casa está lejos del banco. = My house is far from the bank.

en

en = in, and also on.

Examples:

  • Pongo mi ordenador en la mesa. = I put my computer on the table.
  • Pongo mi cartera en el coche. = I put my wallet in the car.

en coche, a pie

To say how you go somewhere, use en.

  • en coche = by car (literally: in car)
  • en tren = by train
  • en bici = by bike
  • a pie = on foot (literally: at foot)

Examples:

  • Voy en tren. = I go by train.
  • Voy a pie. = I go on foot.

Typical mistakes:

  • Voy a tren. → incorrect
  • Voy en tren. → correct
