voy a, ir and venir

voy a + verb

voy a + verb = I'm going to + verb.

  • voy a = I'm going to
  • vas a = you're going to
  • va a = he/she/it is going to

Examples:

  • Mañana voy a volver a casa temprano. = Tomorrow I'm going to come back home early.
  • Mañana va a llover. = Tomorrow it's going to rain.
  • Mañana va a hacer calor. = Tomorrow it's going to be hot.

In va a hacer, three short words come in a row.
Spanish speakers say them almost as one word.

a: going somewhere

a (to) goes before the place.

a + el = al.

Examples:

  • Voy a casa. = I'm going home.
  • Voy al banco. = I'm going to the bank.

Typical mistakes:

  • Voy a el banco. → incorrect
  • Voy al banco. → correct

ir and venir

  • venir = to come: only if you are at the destination. Think come here.
  • ir = to go: if you are not at the destination. Think go there.

When you say yes to an invitation, you are not there yet, so it is voy (I go), not vengo.

Examples:

  • ¿Quieres venir a mi casa? = Do you want to come to my house?
  • Sí, voy. = Yes, I'm coming. (literally: I go)
  • Luego voy. = I'm coming later. (literally: later I go)
