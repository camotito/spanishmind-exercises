siempre, a veces, nunca

How often

  • siempre = always
  • a veces = sometimes (literally: at times)
  • nunca = never

siempre and a veces can go at the beginning or at the end of the sentence.

Examples:

  • Siempre desayuno a las diez. = I always have breakfast at ten.
  • A veces voy en tren. = Sometimes I go by train.
  • Voy en tren a veces. = Sometimes I go by train.
  • Nunca pongo leche en el café. = I never put milk in my coffee.
  • ¿Qué no comes nunca? = What do you never eat?
