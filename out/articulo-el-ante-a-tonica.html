El instead of la before a stressed a-

The rule

Feminine singular nouns that start with a stressed a- or ha- take el (and un), but they stay feminine.

  • El agua está fría. / The water is cold.
  • El hampa, el ancla, el álgebra, el hacha.
  • In the plural the feminine comes back: las aguas, las hachas, las águilas.

Traps

  • The stress must fall on the first syllable: la almohada (stress on hoa), la abeja (stress on be), las amapolas.
  • An adjective in between: la amable ama de llaves.
  • Nouns for women: la árbitra. Letters: la "a".

Other cases

  • No article in tener hambre (hambre is stressed on ham-, but there is no article here).
  • Uncountable nouns after a negative existence verb: No hay agua.
  • Demonstratives stay feminine: esta agua, aquella hacha, esa área, aquellas alas.
