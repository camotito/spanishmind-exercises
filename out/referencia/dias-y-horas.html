Days, hours and meals

Say when you do things.

Days of the week

  • lunes = Monday
  • martes = Tuesday
  • miércoles = Wednesday
  • jueves = Thursday
  • viernes = Friday
  • sábado = Saturday (think Sabbath)
  • domingo = Sunday

All the days are masculine, so they go with el (the).

Spanish has no word for on here. Just el.

  • el lunes = on Monday (literally: the Monday)
  • el sábado = on Saturday
  • los martes = on Tuesdays

Examples:

  • El lunes trabajo a las nueve. = On Monday I work at nine.
  • Tengo clase los martes y los viernes. = I have class on Tuesdays and Fridays.

Typical mistakes:

  • En lunes trabajo. → incorrect
  • El lunes trabajo. → correct
  • No puedo venir al sábado. → incorrect
  • No puedo venir el sábado. → correct

Hours

Hours always come with la or las (the).

  • ¿A qué hora...? = At what time...? (literally: at what hour)
  • a las diez = at ten
  • a las nueve y media = at half past nine (literally: at the nine and a half)

Examples:

  • ¿A qué hora desayunas? = What time do you have breakfast?
  • Desayuno a las diez. = I have breakfast at ten.
  • ¿A qué hora vuelves a casa hoy? = What time are you coming back home today?

The meals

  • desayunar = to have breakfast
  • comer = to eat, and also to have lunch
  • cenar = to have dinner
  • el desayuno = breakfast
  • la comida = lunch, and also food
  • la cena = dinner
