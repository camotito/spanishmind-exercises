💡 Remember that 'décimo' means tenth and agrees in gender with the masculine noun 'ordenador'.

Completa la frase: 'Vosotros cogéis el ___ ascensor' (1st).

💡 'Primero' drops the final '-o' before a masculine singular noun like 'ascensor'.

Fix the error: Este es mi tercero ordenador portátil.

💡 The ordinal 'tercero' drops the final '-o' (becomes 'tercer') when placed directly before a masculine singular noun.

Fix the error: Vosotros cogéis la primero opción.

💡 Ordinal numbers must agree in gender with the noun they modify. 'Opción' is feminine, so we use 'primera'.
